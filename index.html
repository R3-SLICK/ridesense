How this connects to a FreedConn R‑series camera →
FreedConn R‑series units (R1 Plus, R1 Pro, R3 Pro) broadcast their own Wi‑Fi hotspot and are read by FreedConn's own RoadCam app — there's no public SDK for third‑party live‑feed access today. This prototype runs its detection pipeline on your phone's camera as a stand‑in for that feed. If your unit's Wi‑Fi module exposes a raw MJPEG/HTTP stream URL, you can point RideSense at it directly via Helmet Cam Wi‑Fi above. All detection runs on‑device — no video ever leaves your phone.

This is a driver‑assist prototype, not a certified safety device. Distance and closing‑speed estimates are approximated from bounding‑box size and are not calibrated to your camera's optics. Mount your phone outside your primary sightline, rely on audio callouts rather than glancing at the screen, and always ride to your own judgement and local law.
